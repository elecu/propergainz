About Me

I have a PhD in Physics and hold certifications as a Professional Weight Training Coach and Sports Nutritionist from WABBA International Bodybuilding and Fitness Limited. I combine scientific rigor with practical coaching for vegetarian/vegan athletes and fitness enthusiasts

Having been a strict vegetarian for over 13 years, I deeply understand how to optimize physical performance and body composition on a plant based diet. I immersed myself in sports nutrition to master the balance between long-term health and athletic performance. While these principles guided my own transformation, I know every body is different; that is why I focus on designing tailored strategies to help other vegetarians and vegans achieve their specific fitness goals and build the body they want to attain
